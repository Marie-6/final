# Konbini District: Content Draft

Ready to drop into the dialogue format described in `docs/architecture.md`. Skill focus: numbers and prices, quick yes/no service questions, and the famous konbini routine.

**Scenario:** the player buys an お弁当 (bento) and a drink with their remaining yen. Tanaka-sensei tops them up to ¥700 after the lesson.

## Prep lesson (school)

| Japanese | Romaji | English | Chinese |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| お弁当 | obentō | boxed lunch | 便当 |
| 袋 | fukuro | bag | 袋子 |
| 温めます | atatamemasu | (I'll) heat it up | 加热 |
| お願いします | onegaishimasu | please (do it) | 拜托了 |
| 大丈夫です | daijōbu desu | I'm fine (no thanks) | 不用了 |
| そのままで | sono mama de | as it is | 就这样 |
| ろっぴゃく | roppyaku | 600 | 六百 |
| はっぴゃく | happyaku | 800 | 八百 |

**Lesson note (both tracks):** in shops, 大丈夫です is a polite "no, thank you."
**Lesson note (Chinese track):** 大丈夫 is a false friend. In Chinese it means "a real man"; in Japanese it means "okay" or "no thanks."
**Lesson note (numbers):** 300 is さんびゃく, 600 is ろっぴゃく, 800 is はっぴゃく. The sound changes are the hard part.

## Dialogue

**k1. Clerk:** いらっしゃいませ！
Irasshaimase! / Welcome! / 欢迎光临！
(Next. The player picks a bento and a drink from the shelves, then comes to the till.)

**k2. Clerk:** お弁当、温めますか？
Obentō, atatamemasu ka? / Shall I heat up your bento? / 便当要加热吗？

| Answer | Keys | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| はい、お願いします。 (Hai, onegaishimasu.) | お願い, はい | Correct, go to k3 |
| いいえ、そのままで。 (Iie, sono mama de.) | そのまま, いいえ | Correct, go to k3 |
| ください。 (Kudasai.) | ください | Soft mistake. Clerk: えっと…温めますか？ (Etto… atatamemasu ka?) Tip: this is a yes/no question. Answer with はい、お願いします or いいえ. |

**k3. Clerk:** 袋はご利用ですか？
Fukuro wa go-riyō desu ka? / Would you like a bag? / 需要袋子吗？

| Answer | Keys | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| はい、お願いします。 | お願い, はい | Correct, +¥3 bag fee added to the price |
| いいえ、大丈夫です。 | 大丈夫, いいえ | Correct |
| 袋をください。 (Fukuro o kudasai.) | 袋 | Correct (also natural) |

**k4. Clerk:** ポイントカードはお持ちですか？
Pointo kādo wa o-mochi desu ka? / Do you have a point card? / 您有积分卡吗？

| Answer | Keys | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| いいえ。 (Iie.) | いいえ, ない, ありません | Correct |
| はい。 (Hai.) | はい | Soft mistake. Clerk waits for a card, then the player pats their empty pockets. Tip: you don't have one! Say いいえ. This question is asked in almost every konbini. |

**k5. Clerk (listening test, romaji hidden):** ろっぴゃくはちじゅう円です。
Roppyaku hachijū-en desu. / That's 680 yen. / 一共 680 日元。

| Coin button | Result |
| --- | --- |
| ¥608 | Soft mistake: はちじゅう (hachijū) is 80, not 8. |
| ¥680 | Correct, go to k6 (¥683 if the bag was chosen; the clerk says ろっぴゃくはちじゅうさん円) |
| ¥860 | Soft mistake: you heard ろっぴゃく (roppyaku), which is 600, not 800. |

**k6. Clerk:** ありがとうございました！またお越しくださいませ。
Arigatō gozaimashita! Mata okoshi kudasaimase. / Thank you very much! Please come again. / 非常感谢！欢迎再次光临。
(End. Player receives the bento in hand and the コンビニ station stamp.)
