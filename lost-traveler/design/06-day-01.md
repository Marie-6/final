# Day 1: 迷子 (Lost)

Nov 20, 2026 · @Marie

Day 1 scenes and decisions. Rules, characters and style live in Lost Traveler: Game Rules.

## Overview

About 10 to 15 minutes, five scenes, story only.

- **Switched on:** main tasks, the lesson (required), the diary.
- **Switched off:** daily tasks, hidden discovery, night exploring. Bedtime follows check-in.

### Scene 1: The bench (朝 morning)

- **Where:** a bench outside みなと駅 (Minato Station). The game opens with the player already sitting on it, back to the station and just beside the 東口 (east exit) door, looking up at the station sign. Pressing E stands them up.
- **Goal:** Tanaka-sensei, your new teacher, has been looking for you. Introduce yourself so he knows you're his student.
- **Key lines:** 大丈夫ですか？ (Are you okay?), はじめまして。〜です。 (Nice to meet you. I'm ~.)
- **Soft mistakes:** saying さようなら (goodbye) or ありがとう (thank you) instead of your name.
- **Ends with:** Tanaka lends you ¥500 until your bag turns up and takes you to the school.
- **Transition:** no walk. Tanaka says じゃあ、行きましょう！ (Well then, let's go!), the screen fades to black, and it fades back in on the classroom in the afternoon. The school is too far from the station to walk, and the fade covers the jump from morning to afternoon.

### Scene 2: First lesson (昼 afternoon)

- **Where:** 日本語学校 (the language school).
- **Goal:** learn what you need for the café and the evening.
- **Teaches:** 〜をください, drink words, ホットで / アイスで, 店内で / 持ち帰りで, and the hiragana さ, く, ら.
- **Practice:** say コーヒーをください out loud, then match さくら to its sound.
- **Voice:** the 🔊 Listen buttons use the narrator's voice (Jason) at 0.8x, so each phrase is easy to follow. Tanaka speaks in his own voice (Haruto) outside the lesson.

### Scene 3: The café (昼 afternoon)

- **Where:** the café across the street.
- **Goal:** order a drink and pay.
- **Key lines:** ご注文は？ (What would you like?), ホットとアイス、どちらにしますか？ (Hot or iced?), さんびゃく円です。 (That's 300 yen.)
- **Soft mistakes:** asking where the toilet is, answering はい to a choice question, paying ¥30 or ¥3,000.
- **Ends with:** a drink in hand and the café stamp.
- **Voice:** the café staff speak with Asuka's voice.

### Scene 4: Finding さくら荘 (夜 evening)

- **Where:** the streets around the station, now lit by signs and lanterns.
- **Goal:** Tanaka gives a card that says さくら荘. Find the matching sign among several similar ones, such as さくら書店 (a bookshop), さくま歯科 (a dentist) and くらや (a shop).
- **What it tests:** reading hiragana in the world, not in a lesson.
- **Soft mistake:** knocking on the wrong door gets a friendly reply, such as ここは本屋ですよ (This is a bookshop!).

### Scene 5: Check-in and bedtime (夜 evening)

- **Where:** さくら荘, a small guesthouse.
- **Goal:** introduce yourself to Satō-san, then go to bed.
- **Key lines:** こんばんは (good evening), はじめまして again (reuse), おやすみなさい (good night).
- **Ends with:** the diary, then sleep and save. Satō-san asks you to buy breakfast at the konbini tomorrow morning.

### Day 1 diary

Three sentences about the day, each with one word missing. The player picks the word from the notebook.

- 田中さんは日本語の ___ です。 (Ms. Tanaka is a Japanese ___.) Answer: 先生 (sensei, teacher)
- カフェで ___ をのみました。 (I drank ___ at the café.) Answer: the drink the player ordered
- ___ 荘にとまります。 (I'm staying at ___-sō.) Answer: さくら

## Decisions for you

Each question has my recommendation. Change any answer in place or comment on it.

1. **Can players skip the lesson on Day 1?** Options: required, or skippable.
   - **Decided:** required on Day 1 only, because it is the tutorial. Skippable from Day 2.
2. **Can players keep exploring at night before sleeping?** Options: yes, or bedtime starts right after check-in.
   - **Decided:** yes, from Day 2. On Day 1, bedtime follows check-in, to match the shorter, story-only first day.
3. **How long should Day 1 take?** Options: 10, 15 to 20, or 30 minutes.
   - **Decided:** about 10 to 15 minutes. This is how long Day 1 takes to play, not a timer: nothing in the game counts down. Exploring is saved for later days so Day 1 stays focused on the story.
